# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

> **Draft — counsel review required before publication.** Last updated: 2026-11-24.

This DPA forms part of the agreement between the customer (controller) and NeatSprint (processor)
for the hosted service.

## 1. Scope and roles

NeatSprint processes personal data only to provide the service, on documented instructions, and
only for the duration of the agreement. The customer determines monitoring scope, retention and
user roles.

## 2. Categories of data and data subjects

- Data subjects: customer employees, contractors and invited users.
- Data: account details, work items, time entries, device metadata, screenshots and heartbeats
  where monitoring is enabled, audit logs.

## 3. Processor obligations

- Confidentiality, least privilege and personnel training.
- Technical and organisational measures described in `docs/SECURITY.md`.
- Assistance with data-subject requests, impact assessments and consultations.
- Breach notification without undue delay and within 24 hours of confirmation.
- Deletion or return of data at termination, subject to legal retention.

## 4. Sub-processors

The current list is in `legal/subprocessors.md`. The customer is notified of changes and may
object on reasonable grounds.

## 5. International transfers

Where data leaves the EEA/UK, transfers rely on an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual
Clauses plus supplementary measures. Screenshot encryption keys are held in the configured KMS
region.

## 6. Security incidents

NeatSprint contains the incident, preserves audit evidence, notifies the customer, and provides a
written post-incident report. The customer is responsible for regulator and data-subject
notification for its workspace.

## 7. Audit and deletion

Customers may request a security questionnaire or audit report annually. On termination,
screenshots and derived data are destroyed; work and audit data follow the agreed retention or are
returned in machine-readable form.

## 8. Liability and precedence

Liability follows the main agreement. If this DPA conflicts with the main agreement on data
protection, this DPA prevails.

Annexes: A (parties), B (processing details), C (technical and organisational measures).
