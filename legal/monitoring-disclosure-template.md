## What is monitored

NeatSprint records **screenshots of your work displays** at random intervals **only while your
timer is running**, plus aggregate activity heartbeats (active/idle seconds). It does not capture
keystrokes, audio, camera, clipboard contents, private messages, files or browsing history.

## Why

The workspace uses this to understand delivery effort and to keep time records honest. Monitoring
is not used for covert surveillance or for monitoring private devices.

## Your controls

- A visible indicator shows when capture is active; there is no silent mode.
- Screenshots are visible to you first.
- You can delete any capture inside the privacy window (default 24 hours, configured by your
  workspace). Deletion destroys the image and any derived AI data.
- Managers see a tombstone for a deleted capture, never the image.
- You can view every capture, deletion and monitoring session in **My work → Transparency**.
- You can ask your workspace admin about retention, interval and which displays are captured.

## Retention and access

Screenshots follow the workspace retention setting and are excluded from backups. Access is
limited to you and authorised managers in your team, and every view is written to the audit log.
